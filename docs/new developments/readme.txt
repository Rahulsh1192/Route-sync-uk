RouteSync — Updated Client Explanation

## Overview

RouteSync is a premium AI-powered learning platform designed for UK learner drivers and Approved Driving Instructors (ADIs). It combines route learning, instructor booking, AI-powered learning insights, and synchronized driving videos into one modern mobile and web application.

The platform helps learners build confidence by practising real driving routes, understanding complex road layouts, reviewing instructor-recorded driving videos, and booking verified instructors for practical lessons.

---

# Who Uses RouteSync?

## Learner Drivers

Learners use RouteSync as their complete digital driving companion.

They can:

* Access practice routes for their chosen test centre
* Watch synchronized front and rear driving videos
* Practise routes using interactive map mode
* Receive AI-generated learning summaries
* Track their learning progress
* Book verified ADI (approved driving instructors)
* Access premium learning content through a subscription

---

## Verified ADI Instructors

Verified Approved Driving Instructors (ADIs) use RouteSync to grow and manage their driving business.

They can:

* Create and manage their instructor profile
* Set lesson prices and availability
* Accept and manage learner bookings
* Upload GPX route recordings or record route option for adi( for learners if they dont have video for the moment video can be uploaded later by another adi instructor  ) when we start the app adi user can record map or gpx with start recording map button or upload map 
* the system automatically detects the starting point and the end point and save the route for learners but later with the same point we can record the video and merge it with the map
* Upload front and rear driving videos
* Contribute high-quality learning routes to the platform

If an ADI approved driving instructor wants to use the learner practice routes themselves—for lesson planning, route familiarisation, or training—they can subscribe to the same Premium Route Library available to learners.

This keeps the platform fair while giving instructors optional access to premium learning content.

# Secure ADI Login System

To protect instructor identity, uploaded content, and platform security:

* One ADI licence is linked to one RouteSync account
* One account is linked to one registered email address
* Only one active login session is allowed at a time
* An instructor may log out from one device and log in on another
* Simultaneous logins on multiple devices are not permitted

This prevents account sharing, protects instructor content, and maintains the integrity of the platform.

# Offline Route Access

Premium users can download routes for use without an internet connection.

Downloaded routes are securely linked to the learner's RouteSync account and remain accessible only within the RouteSync application.

The files are encrypted and cannot be exported, copied, or shared outside the app, ensuring that RouteSync content remains protected.
