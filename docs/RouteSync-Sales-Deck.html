1. What is Test Routify?

Test Routify is a UK driving-test learning platform that helps learner drivers pass their practical driving test faster by letting them:

  • Watch the exact roads of their local test centre, filmed from real cars with front and rear cameras
  • Practice those routes as turn-by-turn voice navigation — just like a sat-nav, but for their test route
  • Learn the tricky junctions, roundabouts and manoeuvres before they even sit in a test car

 

Think of it as Netflix for driving test routes — but instead of films, you're watching real drives through the exact roads you'll be tested on.

2. The Problem We Solve

For Learner Drivers

Every year, roughly 1.7 million people take the UK practical driving test. The pass rate hovers around 46% — meaning more than half fail, often paying £62+ to rebook.

Why do people fail?

  • They don't know the test routes
  • They've never driven certain junctions or roundabouts before
  • Nerves kick in because everything feels unfamiliar

 

Test Routify fixes this by making the test route familiar before test day. When learners have already virtually "driven" the route dozens of times, test day becomes familiar territory, not a frightening unknown.

For Driving Instructors (ADIs)

Approved Driving Instructors spend hours showing pupils the same test routes repeatedly. Test Routify lets instructors:

  • Upload their own route recordings once
  • Have pupils watch and practice independently between lessons
  • Earn recognition, credits, and credibility through a verified instructor badge

4. How It Works — The Simple Story


Instructor films the route
         ↓
Uploads front cam + rear cam + GPS track to Test Routify
         ↓
Test Routify's AI processes it automatically
(syncs video to GPS, checks quality, removes faces & plates)
         ↓
Route is published on the platform
         ↓
Learner finds the route for their test centre
         ↓
Watches the real drive. Practises with voice guidance.
         ↓
Goes into the test knowing exactly where to turn.

The magic is automatic GPS synchronisation — the app knows exactly where the car is at every moment in the video, so it can show you the map view alongside the dashcam footage, and can fire turn instructions at precisely the right moment during voice practice.

5. Key Features Explained

5.1 — Route Discovery

What it does: A browseable catalogue of driving test routes, searchable by test centre name, town, postcode, or difficulty level.

Why it matters for clients: Users can instantly find routes relevant to their local test centre (e.g. "Mill Hill", "Hendon", "NW7"). Routes are tagged by difficulty — beginner, intermediate, advanced, and test standard — so learners can progress at their own pace.

5.2 — Multi-View Video Player

What it does: Plays the recorded route video with four viewing modes:

  • Front camera — driver's-eye view of the road ahead
  • Rear camera — view out the back window
  • Split view — both cameras side by side simultaneously
  • Live map — the GPS track drawn on a real map, showing the car's position in real time

 

Why it matters for clients: Learners don't just watch a video — they experience the route from multiple angles simultaneously. The split view is particularly powerful: "watch the rear camera when you reverse — you'll know exactly what to look for."

5.3 — Practice Mode with UK Voice Guidance

What it does: A sat-nav-style practice session where the app reads out turn-by-turn instructions in British English as you mentally drive the route — with no video, just like the real test.

Example voice prompts:

"In 200 metres, turn left"
"At the roundabout, take the third exit"
"Bear right at the junction"

Why it matters for clients: This is the killer feature. Learners can close their eyes and "mentally drive" the route at home, at 2am, as many times as they like. The muscle memory and route knowledge builds without spending a penny on extra lessons.

5.4 — GPS-Synchronised Timeline

What it does: A scrubber bar at the bottom of the video shows exactly where along the route the car is. Junctions and roundabouts are marked as visual waypoints on the timeline — click one and jump straight to that moment in the video.

Why it matters for clients: No more scrubbing through 20 minutes of footage looking for "that tricky roundabout". Every junction is bookmarked.

5.5 — Route Upload & Contributor System

What it does: Any user (especially instructors) can upload their own route recordings. They provide:

  • Front dashcam footage (one or more video clips)
  • Rear dashcam footage (optional but recommended)
  • A GPX file (GPS track from a phone or dashcam)

 

What happens automatically after upload:

1. Videos are stitched together if filmed in multiple clips

2. GPS track is validated for accuracy and signal quality

3. Video is synced to the GPS data frame by frame

4. Duplicate routes are detected and flagged

5. AI privacy protection removes faces and number plates

6. A quality score (0–100) is calculated

7. An admin reviews and approves the route

8. Route goes live for all users to watch and practise

Why it matters for clients: The platform grows itself. Instructors are incentivised to contribute because they earn credits, reputation points, and badges. More instructors = more routes = more learners = more revenue.

5.6 — Verified Instructor Programme

What it does: Driving instructors can submit their ADI (Approved Driving Instructor) number and evidence of qualification. Once verified by the Test Routify admin team, they receive:

  • ✅ A "Verified Instructor" badge on their profile
  • 🔼 A search ranking boost (their routes appear higher in results)
  • ⚡ Fast-tracked route approvals
  • 🏅 Instructor reputation bonus in the community leaderboard

 

Why it matters for clients: Learners trust routes from verified instructors more than anonymous uploads. Instructors have a strong incentive to join the platform officially. This creates a quality-controlled, trusted content library.

5.7 — Badges & Reputation System

What it does: Contributors earn badges and build reputation through activity:

Badge How to Earn It
🏅 First Route Publish your first route
🥈 10 Routes Publish 10 routes
🥇 50 Routes Publish 50 routes
⭐ High Quality Consistently score above the quality threshold
🎓 Verified Instructor Verified ADI status confirmed by admin

Reputation scores are displayed on contributor profiles and leaderboards.

Why it matters for clients: Gamification drives engagement. Instructors and contributors stay active because they're building a visible reputation on the platform. Leaderboards create healthy competition.

5.8 — Route Quality Scoring (AI-Powered)

What it does: Every uploaded route is automatically scored 0–100 based on:

  • GPS quality — signal continuity, accuracy, no teleports or drift
  • Video quality — no black frames, corruption, or freezing
  • Sync confidence — how well the video and GPS align
  • Overall composite score

 

Routes with scores below the threshold are flagged for admin review rather than auto-published.

Why it matters for clients: Quality is automatic and objective. Users can trust that published routes are genuinely good quality. The platform maintains its reputation without manual review of every frame.

Why it matters for clients: GDPR and privacy compliance is non-negotiable. Instructors don't need to manually edit their footage. The platform handles it.

10. How to Onboard a New User (Step-by-Step)

For a Learner Driver

Step 1 — Create an Account

  • Visit the Test Routify web app or download the mobile app
  • Click "Create an account"
  • Enter: Display name, Email address, Password
  • Click "Create account" — you're in immediately, no email verification needed to get started

 

Step 2 — Browse Routes

  • You land on the Test Centres page — open a centre, or go to Discover Routes for the full grid
  • Each card shows the route title, test centre location, difficulty, a thumbnail, and the instructor byline

 

Step 3 — Find Your Test Centre

  • Use the global search on the Discover Routes list (no separate search page)
  • Type a route title, instructor name, test centre, town, or postcode (e.g. "Hendon" or "NW4")
  • Results filter as you type

 

Step 4 — Watch a Route

  • Click any route card to open the Route Detail page
  • Click "▶ Watch route"
  • The video player opens — use the view buttons at the top to switch between Front / Rear / Split / Map
  • Use the scrubber bar at the bottom to jump to specific points
  • Junction and roundabout markers are shown above the scrubber — click them to jump

 

Step 5 — Practice the Route

  • Go back to Route Detail, click "🧭 Practice route"
  • If you're on a free account, you'll see the upgrade prompt
  • After upgrading to Premium, press Start and listen to the voice instructions
  • Press Stop at any time, then Restart to go again

 

Step 6 — Upgrade to Premium (Optional)

  • Go to Account in the navigation
  • Click "Upgrade"
  • Choose Monthly (£4.99) or Yearly (£39.99) — the subscription is for one specific test centre and is not switchable
  • You're taken to Stripe's secure checkout — enter card details
  • Return to the app — Premium is active immediately for that test centre

 

For a Driving Instructor Wanting to Upload Routes

Step 1 — Create a regular account (same as above)

Step 2 — Go to Contribute

  • Click your Account page
  • Click "Open contributor tools"
  • Or navigate directly to the Contribute section

 

Step 3 — Read and accept the footage licensing agreement

  • Test Routify requires instructors to confirm they have rights to publish the footage
  • This is a one-time click

 

Step 4 — Upload your first route

  • Click "⬆️ Upload a new route"
  • Fill in: Route title, Description (optional)
  • Select your front camera video file(s) — multiple clips are OK, they'll be stitched
  • Select your rear camera video file(s) — optional but recommended
  • Select your GPX file — the GPS track from your dashcam or phone
  • Tick the licensing agreement checkbox
  • Click Upload

 

Step 5 — Watch the processing

  • You're taken to a status page showing each pipeline stage in real time:
  • - Ingest → Sort clips → Gap detection → Merge → GPS validation → Quality score → Sync → Privacy blur → Duplicate check → Ready for review
  • This typically takes a few minutes

 

Step 6 — Await admin approval

  • Once processing completes, the route enters the Review Queue in the admin console
  • A Test Routify moderator reviews and approves it
  • You'll see the route go live in the catalogue

 

Step 7 — Become a Verified Instructor

  • In the Contribute section, click "🎓 Become a verified instructor"
  • Enter your ADI number and upload your evidence document
  • Submit — a Test Routify admin reviews and approves it
  • Your profile gets the ✅ Verified Instructor badge

12. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does it work offline?

Yes — Premium subscribers can download routes for offline use in the mobile app. Great for areas with poor signal or for practising in the car without using data.

Q: Is the footage GDPR compliant?

Yes — all uploaded footage is automatically processed by our AI to blur faces and vehicle number plates before publishing. Uploaders confirm they have rights to the footage.

Q: What cameras do instructors need?

Any dashcam that records video and a GPX track (or a phone with a GPS tracking app running). Front camera is required, rear is recommended. Multiple clips from a single journey are automatically stitched together.

Q: How long does route processing take?

Typically 2–5 minutes for a standard 20-minute route, depending on server load. The status page updates in real time.

Q: Can a driving school manage multiple instructors?

Yes — instructors can each have their own contributor profile. School-level accounts and team management are on the product roadmap.

Q: What happens if a route is rejected?

The admin provides a reason. The instructor can re-upload with improved footage. Common rejection reasons: GPS signal too poor, video quality too low, content policy issue.

Q: Is there a web version as well as the app?

Yes — the full product is available as a responsive web app that works on any browser (desktop, tablet, or mobile). The native iOS/Android app adds offline support and smoother video performance.

Q: How does the £4.99/month compare to lessons?

The average UK driving lesson costs £30–35/hour. A learner who avoids even half a lesson by being better prepared on test day saves 6× the monthly subscription cost in one go.

Q: Can learners use it on their phone during a lesson?

Not during the driving lesson for safety reasons, but between lessons — at home, on public transport, the night before the test — absolutely.

Q: What is the Community Fund?

10% of Test Routify's net profit is allocated to the Instructor Community Fund. This pot is distributed to verified instructor contributors as a reward for building the platform's content library. All transactions are publicly visible — full transparency.
